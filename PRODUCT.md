# Suro

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Café guests browse the Georgian, English, and Russian menu. Loyalty members keep a permanent digital card. Baristas scan cards and confirm purchases; administrators manage programs and staff.

## Product Purpose

Extend Suro's existing menu with passwordless customer loyalty enrollment, independent stamp programs, rewards, automatically remembered live public cards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user selected Convex for all loyalty storage and authentication rather than the attached brief's Supabase stack. Preserve the existing menu and editor. Membership QR identifiers identify a membership and never authorize changes. Default stamps expire after six calendar months of program-specific inactivity; earned rewards have a separate lifecycle. The card page shows only the branded card, stamps, rewards and QR, with only a quiet Terms disclosure beside the expiry. No paid Wallet issuer integration. No native app.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Suro logo, botanical identity, dark green surfaces, cream text, and teal controls.
